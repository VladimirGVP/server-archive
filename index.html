server-archive

server-archive is a private utility used by its owner to back up data from the owner's own server to the owner's own Google Drive (rclone, scope drive.file). It is not offered to other users.

Privacy policy

The app accesses only files it creates in the owner's Google Drive. No data is shared with, sold to or transferred to third parties. No analytics, no advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: infosaosd@gmail.com
